About

I’m an Electronic Design Engineer at Tesla Energy focused on building and scaling test infrastructure for next-generation energy products. My work spans electrical design, rapid prototyping, high-power systems, and lab development, where I help transform early-stage concepts into reliable real-world systems ready for product launch.

I enjoy solving complex technical problems with practical engineering solutions, combining strong system-level thinking with hands-on execution. Outside of engineering, I competitively race sailboats, previously sailed on the Canadian National Team, and completed a transatlantic ocean crossing.

Sailing

Competitive Sailing

Sailing has been a major part of my life and continues to shape how I approach competition, teamwork, and resilience. I previously competed on the Canadian National Sailing Team and was ranked 2nd overall in North America in the Laser class in 2021, while holding the top U21 ranking.

I also earned multiple Ontario and Quebec Provincial Championship titles between 2018 and 2020. Today, I continue racing competitively in San Francisco aboard J/105s within one of the most competitive fleets in the country.

Atlantic Crossing

One of the most impactful experiences of my life was completing a transatlantic crossing from Antigua to the Azores alongside my father and an experienced crew of sailors.

Living on a four-hours-on, four-hours-off watch schedule for 10 days while racing across the Atlantic created unforgettable moments — from bioluminescent dolphins glowing beneath the boat at night to navigating massive storms and heavy offshore conditions.

Special thanks to Britannia Yacht Club in Ottawa, Ontario, where my sailing journey began.
